As we race towards our algorithmic demise, we may stop for a moment of reflection and wonder...
was it really worth it?

Broken Spirit can be seen less as an album but as an era, a period of great dejection, instability, growth, love, and acceptance. It is simply a collection of songs and video game experience that were created as an attempt to re-connect with the world, with the physical, with life itself and the possibility of hope. I have decided to include many of the demos and failed experiments that led to this project taking shape as an ode to the process and a means of tracing the journey.

Reclaiming our personal autonomy is paramount to defeating the algorithm, the systems of control, and the future that has been imagined for us, this much is known. And so, here is my offering, something that exists in the hands of the people, this project is yours, it belongs to you free from any algorithm, it is yours to do with as you please.

You can download the project and listen to it locally either on mobile or desktop, you can download the game on Mac/Windows and play with your friends, these files are yours, sell them, pirate them, share them with a friend, their future is yours to shape.
