Broken Spirit

Broken Spirit is the most recent EP from Sloucho. This campaign included creating a video game in Unreal Engine (yep you read that right!) I edited an accompanying promotional trailer, designed, produced and programmed NFC Cards, and deisgned a run of T-Shirts. For this campaign I hosted three Direct-to-Fan Listening Parties where we played the game and experienced the new music for the first time.

Rhinestones

Rhinestones was a collaborative track with Irish rappers Lil Skag + Ahmed with Love. The music video was shot entirely in Unreal Engine. I created a Metahuman for each artist using 3D scans of their faces, designed their clothing in Marvelous Designer and filmed their performances IRL to use as motion capture for their avatars.

Future-Forward

Visual identity for an imagined music showcase called Future Forward. This was part of a design task with the brief "made you look". I decided to blend high and low concept visual styles by contrasting contemporary typefaces and neat composition with emojis to create something jarring, bold and visually striking.
