Broken Spirit

As we race towards our algorithmic demise, we may stop for a moment of reflection and wonder... was it really worth it?

Broken Spirit can be seen less as an album but as an era, a period of great dejection, instability, growth, love, and acceptance. It is simply a collection of songs that were created as an attempt to re-connect with the world, with the physical, with life itself and the possibility of hope.

Reclaiming our personal autonomy is paramount to defeating the algorithm, the systems of control, and the future that has been imagined for us, this much is known. And so, here is my offering, something that exists in the hands of the people, this project is yours, it belongs to you free from any algorithm, forever.
